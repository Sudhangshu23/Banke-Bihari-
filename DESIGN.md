## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-luxury boutique hospitality and creative workspace aesthetic. It blends mid-century Milanese architectural discipline with organic Japanese-Nordic warmth and sun-drenched cafe culture. The experience caters to culinary epicures, traveling executives, remote creatives, and discerning patrons who value refined craft, artisanal coffee, and serene, tactile environments.

The visual style embodies **Architectural Warm Minimalism**:
- Generous, purposeful negative space reminiscent of raw linen menus, honed travertine, and warm microcement surfaces.
- Pristine typographic contrast anchored by geometric architectural letterforms, delicate line weights, and refined structural alignments over artificial ornamentation.
- Organic serenity with tactile presence: digital touchpoints reproduce the weight of letterpress stationery, brushed brass details, and roasted espresso tones.

## Colors

The color palette enforces a strict, warm-editorial hierarchy calibrated around natural roasted coffee beans, parchment, and raw earthen architecture:

- **Main Website Background (`#E8DDCA` - Warm Ivory Cream):** The foundational canvas layer across all pages, offering an organic, glare-free, hospitable baseline.
- **Cards & Section Containers (`#F8F4EC` - Soft White):** Elevated surfaces, menu blocks, food cards, and workspace booking modules that sit proudly atop the ivory background.
- **Secondary Background & Structural Lines (`#C1B4A4` - Warm Beige):** Delicate hairline borders, neutral iconography, table dividers, card outlines, and inactive control boundaries.
- **Interactive Accents & Primary CTA (`#A87850` - Caramel Brown):** Reserved for primary interactive states, buttons, links, active tab indicators, and booking selections.
- **Headings & Titles (`#70452F` - Coffee Brown):** Editorial display typography, section headers, categories, and signature wordmark treatments.
- **Dark Accents, Body Text & Deep Contrast (`#39271F` - Espresso Brown):** Narrative body prose, deep contrast footers, icon fills, and high-impact structural anchors.

Pure `#000000` and clinical cool white (`#FFFFFF`) are strictly avoided; pure white is restricted exclusively to frosted image overlays and luminous modal badges.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy echoes the brand's geometric, architectural identity:

- **Headings & Display (Space Grotesk):** Features clean architectural forms, distinctive open counters, and high-impact geometry that directly mirror the custom letterforms of the brand emblem. Rendered in Coffee Brown (`#70452F`).
- **Body & Culinary Prose (DM Sans):** Smooth, low-contrast, and effortlessly readable. Optimized for editorial menus, artisanal coffee tasting notes, ingredients, and venue descriptions. Rendered in Espresso Brown (`#39271F`).
- **Brand Subtitles, Wayfinding & Micro-actions (Outfit):** Pure geometric sans-serif with extended letter-spacing. Used for the signature `cafe • eatery • workspace` lockup, navigation items, buttons, and micro-labels. Always styled with deliberate tracking (`0.12em` to `0.22em`) to mirror luxury boutique signage.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to evoke editorial print journals and spacious gallery layouts:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, max-width 1380px, `3rem` (48px) margins, and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Uses intentional asymmetry—pairing 7-column architectural photography with 5-column menu narratives.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `2rem` (32px) margins, and `1.25rem` (20px) gutters. Two-column menu groupings flex evenly or shift into alternating layouts.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) margins, and `1rem` (16px) gutters. Content flows into a relaxed, linear reading rhythm with `space-xl` separators between sections to prevent visual clutter.

Spacing intervals are calculated in modular units of 4px and 8px, placing a premium on breathing room, horizontal alignment, and balanced white space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **material surface contrast, fine hairline boundaries, and subtle ambient warmth** rather than synthetic shadows:

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** `#E8DDCA` (Warm Ivory Cream) serves as the foundational floor plane.
- **Card & Content Surfaces:** `#F8F4EC` (Soft White) cards float naturally on top of the canvas, defined by clean 1px borders in Warm Beige (`#C1B4A4`).
- **Ambient Elevation:** Floating menus, popovers, and workspace booking modals use an extra-diffused shadow tinted with warm Espresso: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -10px rgba(57, 39, 31, 0.08)`.
- **Glassmorphism & Overlays:** Sticky navigation bars and floating order bars utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)` with an 85% opacity `#E8DDCA` or `#F8F4EC` fill, framed by a bottom 1px hairline border in `#C1B4A4`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft architectural structure (roundedness: 1)**:
- Standard interactive elements—buttons, card containers, inputs, and imagery frames—use subtle `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corners, expressing the crisp elegance of cut linen, stone tile counters, and architectural paper.
- Fully rounded pill forms are reserved strictly for the brand’s infinity-loop emblem, status chips, and circular quantity steppers. Main UI cards and call-to-action blocks retain architectural rectangular proportions with softened edges.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action:** Solid Caramel Brown (`#A87850`) background with Soft White (`#F8F4EC`) text. Styled in `label-button` with uppercase tracking (`0.14em`). 48px height, `space-lg` horizontal padding, 4px border-radius. On hover, deepens smoothly to Coffee Brown (`#70452F`) with a 200ms ease.
- **Secondary / Architectural:** Clean Soft White (`#F8F4EC`) surface with a 1px border in Coffee Brown (`#70452F`) and text in `#70452F`. On hover, background shifts to Warm Ivory Cream (`#E8DDCA`).
- **Ghost / Text Button:** Unbordered Coffee Brown (`#70452F`) with a 1px baseline underline in Caramel Brown (`#A87850`), animated on hover.

### Cards & Menu Containers
- **Surface:** Rendered in Soft White (`#F8F4EC`) surrounded by a 1px solid border in Warm Beige (`#C1B4A4`).
- **Internal Spacing:** `space-lg` (28px) padding.
- **Typography Layout:** Section headers in Coffee Brown (`#70452F`), culinary details in Espresso Brown (`#39271F`), pricing indicators in Caramel Brown (`#A87850`).
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** Subtle border tint shift to Caramel Brown (`#A87850`) with a smooth 1px vertical translation.

### Chips, Badges & Origin Markers
- Background in Warm Ivory Cream (`#E8DDCA`) with a 1px border in Warm Beige (`#C1B4A4`). Text in Coffee Brown (`#70452F`) styled in `label-caption`. Height: 24px, horizontal padding: 10px, 4px radius. Used for "Pour Over", "Single Origin", "Quiet Workspace", and "Wifi 6".

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Soft White (`#F8F4EC`) with a 1px continuous border in Warm Beige (`#C1B4A4`).
- Text in Espresso Brown (`#39271F`), placeholder in `#C1B4A4`.
- Focus State: 1px border transitions to Caramel Brown (`#A87850`) with no harsh outer glow.

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkbox)
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px square with 2px radius. Border in `#C1B4A4`; active checked fill in `#A87850` with Soft White check icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18x18px circle. Border in `#C1B4A4`; active state displays an inner solid dot in Caramel Brown (`#A87850`).

### Workspace & Table Booking Slots
- **Idle Slots:** Soft White (`#F8F4EC`) tiles with 1px Warm Beige (`#C1B4A4`) border and Espresso Brown time label.
- **Selected Slots:** Solid Espresso Brown (`#39271F`) fill with Soft White (`#F8F4EC`) text and a Caramel Brown (`#A87850`) indicator accent.

### Footer & Grounding Layer
- Deep Espresso Brown (`#39271F`) foundation. Brand wordmark and headlines in Warm Ivory Cream (`#E8DDCA`), secondary links and hours in Warm Beige (`#C1B4A4`).
